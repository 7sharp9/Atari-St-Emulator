## Known traps

- **A directory named `build/` is gitignored** (`.gitignore:52`): `py/build/` stayed untracked until renamed `py/worldbuild/` (now in CLAUDE.md).
- **`callcap` presets are hex**: `D0=10` is `$10`. fsm15 reported "slot 5's eyes never draw" from that and retracted it; `$1699e` was also called not callcapable from a listing that started two bytes early (its `movem.l` push is its own first instruction).
- **`gate_coverage.py` counts a cite, not an execution**: `$15c46` runs in the fsm15 gate through mode `$5a` and still lists as ungated. Census with `hits` before calling a routine unproven.
- **`callcap` outcome `THREW ... WriteEa: immediate operand is not a valid destination`** is a runaway (an odd-address word access from a poked group that does not form a valid group, or a `bpc` hit that is not a call), not an emulator bug: four synthetic `fsm15` states throw it and are the "5 unrun".
- **A model gate caches callcap JSON**: a `reuse` run keeps stale deltas after a case builder changes; delete the state's JSON or run without `reuse` (the promoted gates here were all rerun without it).
- **`bpc <addr> <n> <max>` counts hits from the start snapshot, and `max` bounds the whole gap** (`capture_hits.py --max` defaults to 50M); a hit it does not reach still leaves a `.snap` with no `.ram`: filter on `.ram`. **`bpc` gives up after 200000 steps by default**; a world build is 2.5 M steps and `$238c` is reached after about 8 M.
- **Transcribe from the listing, not from a similar neighbour**; **a failure branch that always follows another failure is a clue** (tag the model's arms by cause and count); **`add.w`/`subi.w` + `bge/blt` test N xor V**, so a wrapped word's sign is wrong (fsm15 found 211 edge-state failures from it).
- **macOS `seq -s,` leaves a trailing comma** (`capture_hits.py` rejects it); **the Edit tool drops trailing spaces from `new_string`**; **bucket links of records below `$51b66` are negative 16-bit offsets** (`_objaddr`).
- Earlier traps still apply: a routine's tail that ends in the iterator's `bra $1622c` cannot be `callcap`ed in place; REPL `w` takes longwords at even addresses; subagent reports need one spot check (this pass: every headline gate reproduced from cache, then rerun fresh from the promoted location); entity records are the first 512 only; one entity tick is ~200k steps; `py/clicks.py` pointer moves 1:1 (start `hits` before `mouse down`); do not reopen the SingleStepTests track or `$4342`'s 8 proven branches; a full `pm_export.py` rerun regresses `entities.json`.
- Port traps (`port/stepper`): `--shot`/`--selfcheck` prove one instant (use `--playtest`); the port stays a renderer by Dave's decision (no entity simulation; `Equipment.fs` stays uncalled); the `port/` and `tools/pm_fsm_ref.py` identifiers still say herd/shepherd, morale, survivability: rename only with a gate rerun.
