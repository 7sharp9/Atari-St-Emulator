## Known traps

- **A door-graph chain is a hypothesis about geometry** (CLAUDE.md): drive a leg before building on it; the emulator makes a 46-leg chain a 40-second run.
- **`hold` ends after six stalled chunks**, which is shorter than some door waits (a hound in the doorway): `Z<door>:<lane>:<chunk>` raises the chunk. A door needs the whole bbox inside its portal range; the lane is the hero's lead coordinate. `M<door>` searches lanes on forks, `X<door>` a standing wait.
- **zsh redirects an unquoted `GR:x>=24`:** quote every `explore.py` command containing `>` or `<`.
- **A render right after a room change shows the old room** for about 100,000 steps; `Q` (probe) leaves the hero airborne when nothing is in front: never save a snapshot you will start a chain from right after `Q`.
- **Health alone does not show a POISON bite:** the ticks (-10 each) come in later legs; check `2434(A5)` (`N` in `explore.py`).
- **`slot_map()` in `g2/lib.py` starts at slot 1 and skips slot 0 (the first rucksack item)**: read `type8(r)['recs']`.
- **`watch` on an odd byte can miss a covering word write** (open item 5a): watch the even byte below as well before writing "nothing writes X".
- **`timeout` does not exist on macOS:** a check piped through it fails before running and its `grep -c` prints 0. Do not `pkill` by pattern (denied): stop your own run with TaskStop.
- **`H.obj()` in the verbs2/graph harness goes stale after a DELETE** (deletion compacts the resource block; read the live index entry); poking a type-8 record needs its index entry.
- **Two snapshots sit in the `$0117c4` fatal-assert spin and cannot run:** `s86/a7/snaps/gomove31.snap`, `s86/a6/snaps/room69.snap`. Verb 41 PLACE with no free space asserts and hangs the game.
- **Throw range depends on the steps since the last move; hold timing: a hold starts moving about 40,000 steps after the press, one cell per about 25,000 in level 1 (10,000 in level 0); a hold begun while falling never moves; a panel left open freezes every hold; the Return grid with six or more items puts the cursor on a different item than FIRE opens (`find_n`, `C<oid>`).**
- **Earlier standing traps:** injected-state results show a mechanism, not a route (label them); health only goes up by a verb 45; door words are hex door numbers; the REPL prints at most 4096 lines; REPL `w <addr> <8 hex>` writes four bytes; verb 66 with a class other than 10 kills the REPL; `lib/drv.py` does `os.chdir(ROOT)` on import (absolutize snapshot arguments).
